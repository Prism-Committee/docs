## WebShopX

**WebShopX** is a web-first shop system for Paper, Purpur, Spigot, and Folia servers. It combines the official shop, player market, wallets, orders and delivery, recharge, and administration.

**Project and downloads:** [GitHub source](https://github.com/Cc-Cece/WebShopX) · [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/plugin/webshopx) · [MineBBS](https://www.minebbs.com/resources/x-folia-webshopx.15688/)

## WebShopX-Payments

**WebShopX-Payments** is the standalone payment extension for WebShopX and provides concrete payment-channel integrations.

**Project and downloads:** [GitHub](https://github.com/Prism-Committee/WebShopX-Payments) · [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/plugin/wsxpay) · [MineBBS](https://www.minebbs.com/resources/x-folia-webshopx-payments-webshopx.16526/)
